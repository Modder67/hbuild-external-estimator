## What H LEDGER now does (server facts, not to be changed from the estimator side)
 
The switch `ledger.estimator_sync` is controlled by HBUILD staff and is OFF by default. While off:
 
- Every signed delivery from an `estimator` provider account to `POST /api/intake/v1/projects` and
  `POST /api/intake/v1/documents` — including replays of previously finished deliveries — returns
  **HTTP 503** with `Retry-After: 3600` and this envelope:
```json
  { "ok": false,
    "error": { "code": "temporarily_unavailable",
               "message": "Estimator synchronization to H LEDGER is temporarily disabled by the office. Nothing was imported or changed. Keep this delivery in the estimator and retry it after synchronization is re-enabled.",
               "retryable": true,
               "details": { "reason": "estimator_sync_disabled", "setting": "ledger.estimator_sync" } },
    "requestId": "…" }
```
 
  **Detect the paused state by `error.details.reason === "estimator_sync_disabled"`** — never by
  matching message text. A 503 without that marker is an ordinary transient outage and keeps the
  existing backoff behavior.
- Nothing is imported, updated or replayed, and no receipt is stored, so a delivery refused while
  paused applies **exactly once** when resent later with its original Idempotency-Key and body.
  H LEDGER never replays anything by itself; resending is always the estimator's action.
- Creating a new `estimator` provider account returns 409. Existing accounts, mappings, receipts and
  everything already imported are kept; `401 "This provider account was revoked."` still means
  revoked, which is a different, non-recoverable state — do not treat it as paused.
## Requirements
 
1. **Queue, never drop.** Deliveries stay in the estimator's durable outbound queue with their
   original Idempotency-Key and exact signed body. On the paused marker, mark the delivery — and the
   HBUILD connection as a whole — `paused`, not `failed`. Nothing is deleted, regenerated or re-keyed.
2. **Stop the retry loop while paused.** Once the connection is paused, send no further deliveries.
   At most one cheap probe (the oldest queued delivery) per `Retry-After` period, or a manual
   "Check HBUILD sync" action; no exponential-retry hammering. Transient 503s without the marker and
   429s keep the existing bounded backoff honoring `Retry-After`.
3. **Honest UI.** Wherever the estimator shows HBUILD delivery state, show
   "HBUILD sync paused by the office — N deliveries waiting" (with the queue count and the oldest
   item's age). Remove or suppress any "connected", "synced" or "delivery failed" wording for this
   state: paused is neither success nor an estimator error. Estimate authoring, pricing, revisions
   and PDF generation are unaffected and keep working; new estimates queue locally.
4. **No workarounds.** While paused, do not call any other HBUILD endpoint to get records in: no
   manual client/project creation (retired, refuses 409), no new provider accounts (409), no unsigned
   or alternate routes, no direct database access. Keep the account keyId/secret stored as they are.
5. **Resume cleanly.** When a probe succeeds (or staff resume the connection), resend the queue in
   original order — each project's identity delivery before its document deliveries — with the
   original keys and bodies. Expect: previously finished deliveries replay
   (`Idempotent-Replayed: true`); deliveries refused while paused apply once; superseded identity
   deliveries may return `stale` when a newer revision was already sent (send the LATEST revision of
   an estimate that changed while paused; drop only queue items your own newer queued revision of the
   same source record supersedes, per the existing newer-wins rule). Retain source files until each
   document's readback status is `verified`, as the contract already requires.
6. **Distinguish terminal states.** `401` (bad signature or revoked account) and `409`
   (reused key, different body) keep their existing handling — surfaced for attention, never blind-
   retried — and never flip the connection into or out of `paused`.
7. **Per-connection scope.** The pause applies to the HBUILD connection only. If the estimator
   delivers to any other consumer, those paths are untouched.
8. **Update estimator-side records.** Reflect the paused state machine
   (`queued → sent → verified | stale | paused | needs-attention`) in the estimator's own integration
   documentation, and note that while paused, NEW estimator records will not appear in HBUILD.
## Testing (estimator side, no live HBUILD)
 
Against a stub of the intake endpoints:
- The paused marker pauses the connection: queued deliveries stay intact with their keys/bodies;
  no further sends happen except one probe per Retry-After window; UI shows paused with the count.
- A 503 WITHOUT the marker follows ordinary transient backoff and does not pause the connection.
- Resume resends in order (identity first), handles `applied`, `Idempotent-Replayed: true` and
  `stale` correctly, and sends each queued delivery at most once.
- 401 revoked and 409 reused-key surface as attention states, distinct from paused.
- Standalone flows (authoring, pricing, PDFs) run with the connection paused.
Run the estimator's existing test suite and production build; fix failures without weakening checks.
