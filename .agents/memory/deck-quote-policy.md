---
name: Deck quote policy
description: Why issued Deck quotes differ from legacy browser totals and what counts as a complete takeoff
---

Issued Deck revisions use the selected Deck markup tier on the pre-tax subtotal, not the browser's tax-inclusive legacy total. A fixed takeoff lists only calculated deck boards and explicitly selected, calculated lumber; do not promote old browser PDFs or nominal coverage counts into issued ordering documents. An estimate linked to an existing canonical HBUILD project cannot be issued as a new-job Deck revision.

**Why:** The owner confirmed staff-only, before-tax, immutable-revision handling for Deck, linear PDFs sourced from current Deck measurements and rates, and preservation of legacy browser records for reference only. The browser includes 8.5% tax before markup, which would incorrectly inflate a before-tax issued quote. Nominal coverage counts alongside selected lumber can be mistaken for duplicate order quantities.

**How to apply:** When changing Deck pricing, PDF content, intake eligibility, or quote recovery, keep the selected tier and measured quantities consistent with the Deck form but distinguish browser estimates from issued revisions. Require complete selections for measured lumber at issue time on the server, and preserve the exact frozen snapshot for any delivery attempt.
